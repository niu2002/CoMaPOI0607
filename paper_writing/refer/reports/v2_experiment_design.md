# V2 Experiment Design

V2 covers RQ1 overall effectiveness, RQ2 candidate coverage, RQ3 GeoSemID
quality, RQ4 router load/specialization, RQ5 expert complementarity, RQ6
evidence preservation, RQ7 teacher-student supervision, RQ8 long-tail
robustness, RQ9 efficiency, and RQ10 budget/calibration sensitivity.

All tables and figures are generated from experiments/mock_*.csv by
scripts/generate_mock_figures.py. scripts/check_mock_consistency.py verifies
ranking/recall/error decomposition and Mock labels before PDF compilation.
